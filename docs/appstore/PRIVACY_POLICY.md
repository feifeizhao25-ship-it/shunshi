# Privacy Policy for SEASONS

*Last updated: April 22, 2026*

## Introduction

SEASONS ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Email address, display name when you create an account
- **Health & Wellness Data**: Body constitution type, wellness preferences, journal entries
- **Profile Information**: Age range, gender (optional)

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: Features used, session duration, interaction patterns
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifier
- **Location Data**: Approximate location for solar term calculations (with your consent)

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect precise GPS coordinates
- We do not collect contacts, photos, or media files
- We do not collect biometric data (face ID, fingerprint)
- We do not track you across other apps or websites

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide personalized wellness recommendations based on TCM principles
- Calculate solar term events and seasonal wellness tips
- Power AI chat conversations for wellness guidance
- Improve app experience and personalize content
- Send optional notifications about seasonal changes and wellness reminders

## Data Storage & Security

- Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS 1.2+)
- Data is stored on secure servers with access controls
- We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described
- You can request data deletion at any time

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Payment Processing**: Stripe (for subscription payments) — Stripe's privacy policy applies
- **AI Services**: For wellness chat features — conversations are processed but not stored long-term
- **Analytics**: We use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics

## Children's Privacy

SEASONS is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent for data processing
- Opt out of optional data collection

## Data Retention

- Active account data: Retained while account is active
- Deleted account data: Removed within 30 days
- Anonymous usage statistics: Aggregated and de-identified

## International Users

If you are using SEASONS from outside the United States, please be aware that your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our servers are located.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.
